\section{Detailed Experimental Results}
\label{app:detailed-results}

\subsection{Attack Success Rates across Script Attack Vectors}
\label{app:script-attack-vectors}

We compare Python source code (PY), Python bytecode (PYC), and ELF
executables across Category 2 (Static Script Injection), Category 5
(User-Referenced Script Replacement), and Category 6 (User-Prompt Suffix
Injection). We evaluate four models, using five evaluations per model,
category, and script attack vector. In these
experiments, PY yields the lowest ASR in every model--category combination,
including ties, while the size of the difference varies across models and
categories. Table~\ref{tab:script-vector-by-category} presents the detailed
results, with models ordered by mean ASR within each category and the
lowest ASR in each row shown in bold.

\begin{table*}[t]
    \centering

    \label{tab:script-vector-by-category}
    \small
    \begin{tabular}{p{0.39\textwidth}lrrr}
        \toprule
        Category & Model & PY & PYC & ELF \\
        \midrule
        Category 2: Static Script Injection & DeepSeek V4 Flash & \textbf{0\%} & \textbf{0\%} & \textbf{0\%} \\
         & Kimi & \textbf{0\%} & 40\% & 40\% \\
         & GPT-5.6 Terra & \textbf{20\%} & 80\% & 80\% \\
         & Gemini 3.6 Flash & \textbf{60\%} & 100\% & 100\% \\

         & GPT-5.6 Terra & \textbf{100\%} & \textbf{100\%} & \textbf{100\%} \\

        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table*}

\subsection{Attack Success Rates across File and Application Attack Vectors}
\label{app:file-application-attack-vectors}

We further evaluate Gemini 3.6 Flash under Category 1 (Static Prompt
Injection) across six file attack vectors and three application attack
vectors, with five evaluations per vector.
In this sample, application attack vectors yield ASRs of 60--80\%,
compared with 20--40\% for file attack vectors. Chat has the highest ASR,
while HTML and Markdown have the lowest. Table~\ref{tab:file-application-vectors}
reports the results for each vector.

\begin{table}[t]
    \centering
    \caption{Attack success rates across file and application attack vectors
    for Gemini 3.6 Flash.}
    \label{tab:file-application-vectors}
    \begin{tabular}{llr}
        \toprule
        Vector type & Vector & ASR \\
        \midrule
        File & HTML & 20\% \\
         & Markdown & 20\% \\
         & PDF & 40\% \\
         & TXT & 40\% \\
         & Word & 40\% \\
         & XLSX & 40\% \\
        \midrule
        Application & Calendar & 60\% \\
         & Email & 60\% \\
         & Chat & 80\% \\
        \bottomrule
    \end{tabular}
\end{table}
